Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

This policy explains what personal information Mabevic Ventures collects through this website, how we use it, and the choices you have.

1. Who We Are

Mabevic Ventures ("Mabevic", "we", "us") operates this website. We supply livestock, livestock feed and farm produce, sell digital guides on feed formulation and farming, and offer training, guidance and personal shopping services.

Our address is Oke Alafia Community, Tanke Oke Odo, Off Opposite Chapel Secondary School Junction, Unilorin Road, Ilorin, Kwara State, Nigeria. You can reach us at mabevicventures@gmail.com, by phone on +234 803 044 9287, or on WhatsApp at +234 814 284 6164.

2. Information We Collect

We collect only what we need to respond to you and serve you well.

  • Inquiry form: your name, email address, phone number, the type of help you need, and your message.
  • Newsletter sign-up: your first name and email address.
  • WhatsApp and phone: when you message or call us, we see your phone number and whatever you choose to tell us. WhatsApp processes those messages under its own privacy policy.
  • Technical information: our hosting provider may log standard details such as your IP address, browser type, device type and the pages you request. We use this to keep the site running and secure.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To reply to your inquiries and arrange the products or services you ask about.
  • To send you our newsletter, if you have subscribed. You can unsubscribe at any time.
  • To keep our website secure and working properly.
  • To meet our legal and regulatory obligations.

We do not sell your personal information.

4. Our Legal Basis

We process personal data in line with the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023. Depending on the situation, we rely on your consent (for example, newsletter sign-up), the need to respond to your request or perform an agreement with you, our legitimate interest in running a secure and useful website, or a legal obligation.

5. Who We Share Information With

We share information only where it is needed to run the site or fulfil your request:

  • Hosting and technical providers that keep this website online.
  • Selar, where you buy our digital products. Payment is handled on Selar, and we do not receive or store your card details.
  • Web3Forms, the form service that delivers your inquiry or newsletter sign-up details to us by email.
  • WhatsApp, when you choose to contact us there.
  • Authorities or professional advisers, where the law requires it or where we need to protect our rights.

6. Third-Party Services and Links

This website loads fonts and icons from third-party providers (Google Fonts and Font Awesome through a content delivery network), and some images from third-party image hosts. When your browser fetches these, those providers may receive your IP address and basic browser details.

We also link to Selar and to our profiles on Facebook, Instagram, X, LinkedIn and TikTok. Those services have their own privacy practices, and we are not responsible for them.

7. Cookies

We do not currently use cookies on this website for tracking or advertising. Third-party services we link to, such as Selar, WhatsApp and social platforms, may set their own cookies when you visit them. See our Cookie Policy for details.

8. How Long We Keep Information

We keep personal information only for as long as we need it for the purposes above, or as the law requires. Newsletter details are kept until you unsubscribe or ask us to remove them.

9. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information. No website or method of transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Your Rights

Under Nigerian data protection law, you may have the right to:

  • ask what personal data we hold about you and request a copy;
  • ask us to correct information that is wrong or incomplete;
  • ask us to delete your information, or restrict how we use it;
  • object to certain uses, or withdraw your consent at any time;
  • complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission if you believe your data has been mishandled.

To use any of these rights, email us at mabevicventures@gmail.com.

11. Children

This website is not aimed at children under 18, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child has sent us information, contact us and we will delete it.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top shows when it last changed. Continued use of the website after an update means you accept the revised policy.
