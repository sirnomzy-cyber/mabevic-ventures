Cookie Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

This policy explains whether Mabevic Ventures uses cookies on this website, what third-party services may do, and how you can manage cookies.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that a website can store on your device when you visit it. They are widely used to make websites work, to remember your preferences, and to understand how a site is used.

2. How We Use Cookies

We do not currently set cookies on this website for tracking, analytics or advertising. The site works without them, and we do not use cookies to build a profile of you.

3. Third-Party Services

Some parts of the website involve other companies:

  • Fonts and icons. We load fonts from Google Fonts and icons from Font Awesome through a content delivery network. These providers receive your IP address and basic browser details so they can deliver the files. To our knowledge they do not set tracking cookies through this website, and you can read their own policies for more.
  • Links to other services. When you click through to Selar, WhatsApp, Facebook, Instagram, X, LinkedIn or TikTok, you leave our website. Those services may set their own cookies under their own policies, and we do not control them.

4. Types of Cookies, Explained

So you know what to look for on any website, cookies generally fall into these groups:

  • Strictly necessary: needed for a site to function, such as keeping a basket or a login working.
  • Preference: remember choices like language or layout.
  • Analytics: help a site owner understand how visitors use the site.
  • Advertising: used to show or measure ads. We do not use these.

5. If This Changes

If we later add tools that use cookies, such as visitor analytics, we will update this policy first. Where the law requires your consent, we will ask for it before those cookies are set. You can read about how we handle personal data in our Privacy Policy.

6. Managing Cookies

You can control cookies through your browser settings, usually under Privacy or Security. From there you can block cookies, delete the ones already stored, or be warned before a cookie is set. Blocking all cookies can stop some websites from working properly, including the services we link to.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top shows the latest version.
