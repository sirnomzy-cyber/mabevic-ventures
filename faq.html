About Mabevic

What does Mabevic Ventures do?

We supply livestock, livestock feed and farm produce, sell practical digital guides on feed formulation and farming, and offer training, consultancy and personal shopping services. Our focus is helping farmers improve animal health, growth and productivity through improved feed formulated with precise calculations.

Where are you located and when are you open?

We are at Oke Alafia Community, Tanke Oke Odo, Opposite Chapel Secondary School Junction, Unilorin Road, Ilorin, Kwara State, Nigeria.

Our working hours are Monday to Friday, 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM.

How can I contact you?

Message or call us on +234 814 284 6164 (WhatsApp or calls), email mabevicblog@gmail.com, or use the form on our Contact page.

Buying Products

How do I buy livestock, feed or farm produce?

Open the Shop, find the product you want and tap Inquire on WhatsApp. A message naming that product is prepared for you. We then confirm the price, availability, delivery and payment with you directly.

Why don't the products show prices?

Prices and availability for livestock, feed and farm produce are confirmed directly when you inquire, so the information you get is accurate for the time you want to buy.

Do you deliver?

Delivery depends on the product and where you are. Tell us your location when you message us and we will confirm what is possible as part of your order.

What if I need something that is not listed?

Message us on WhatsApp and tell us what you are looking for. We may be able to source it, or point you to the right product.

Digital Guides

Which digital guides are available?
  • How To Formulate Improved Livestock Feed
  • Tested And Proven Poultry Feed Formulas
  • Snail Farming and Management
  • Exotic and Local Dogs Feed Formulas
  • Attraction Marketing

You can see them all, with prices where shown, in the Shop.

How do I buy a guide?

Select Buy Now on the guide you want. You will be taken to that guide's page on Selar, where you complete payment. Selar handles payment and delivery of the guide, as described on the product page.

Can I get a refund on a digital guide?

Digital purchases go through Selar, so Selar's refund rules and the details on each product page apply. If you need help with anything else, contact us and we will assist where we can.

Do the guides guarantee results?

No. The guides share practical methods, but results depend on your animals, land, climate, inputs and how you manage them, so we cannot guarantee particular yields, growth rates, savings or profits.

Can I share a guide with other people?

Each guide is licensed for your personal use. Please do not copy, share or resell it without our written permission. You can read more in our Terms of Service.

Services and Training

What services do you offer?
  • Agricultural extension, consultancy and rural development
  • Agricultural planning, monitoring and evaluation (APME)
  • Training on how to formulate improved livestock feeds
  • Personal shopping services
  • Infopreneur services

To discuss any of these, get in touch.

What is the Feed Formulation Masterclass?

It is a free class on formulating your own top quality, cost-effective feed. It covers choosing ingredients based on your livestock's needs, combining them in the right ratios, and using nutrient-based calculation to get accurate feeding patterns. You can register from the Free Training section on our homepage.

How does your personal shopping service work?

We aim to be a trusted personal shopper for Africans in the diaspora, sourcing familiar products such as Ayoola Poundo Yam flour, Indomie noodles and Milo. Shops and stores looking for reliable supply can ask too. Message us on WhatsApp with what you need.
